The whole‑market method to track all new listings in Villach/Klagenfurt is to combine three layers:
1) Portal‑level search agents (covering 90% of the market)
2) Agency‑level feeds (covering the remaining 10%)
3) A weekly sweep (to catch anything the algorithms miss)

This gives you complete coverage of the market — not just Willhaben/ImmoScout, but also REMAX, Perkonig, Schick, NPK, etc.
Below is the full system, structured and actionable.

  1. Set up portal‑level search agents (covers ~90% of the market)

These are the only tools that reliably capture almost all new listings.
Each bullet begins with a Guided Link so you can expand any part.

  • Willhaben Suchagent — The single most important source; private sellers + agencies.
    Create 2 agents:

    • “Villach 2‑Zimmer 45–65 m² Kauf”
    • “Klagenfurt 2‑Zimmer 45–65 m² Kauf”

  • ImmoScout24 Suchagent — Best for agency listings and new builds.
    Use filters:

    • Region: Villach + Klagenfurt
    • Price: €150–300k
    • Rooms: 2
    • Features: Lift, Parkplatz

  • Immowelt Suchagent — Captures agencies that don’t list on Willhaben.

  • Der Standard Immobilien Suchagent — Good for private sellers and older buildings.

These four agents alone give you 90%+ coverage of all new listings.

  1. Add agency‑level feeds (captures the missing 10%)

Some agencies delay posting to portals or post only on their own site for 24–72 hours.
These are the agencies that matter in Villach/Klagenfurt:

  • REMAX Villach/Klagenfurt — Large inventory, often first to publish.
  • Perkonig Klagenfurt — Strong in Klagenfurt mid‑range flats.
  • Schick Immobilien Villach — Very active in Völkendorf/Lind.
  • NPK Immobilien — Dominant in Villach.
  • Belvimo Klagenfurt — Good for renovated 2000s buildings.
  • Immobilien Consulting Villach — Often has early postings.

You don’t need to check them daily — twice per week is enough.

  1. Perform a weekly sweep (catches everything the agents miss)

This is the “insurance layer” that ensures you never miss a listing.

  • Search Willhaben manually
    Sort by “Neueste zuerst” and scan the last 48 hours.

  • Search ImmoScout manually
    Sort by “Neu eingestellt”.

  • Check Facebook groups
    Private sellers often post here before portals:

    • “Immobilien Villach Privat”
    • “Immobilien Klagenfurt Privat”

  • Check developer pages
    For rare new builds in Villach/Klagenfurt.

This sweep takes 10–12 minutes per week and guarantees full coverage.

  1. Optional: Build a “priority watchlist” of buildings

Since you already have a shortlist of the best buildings, you can track them directly:

  • Track supply in target buildings
    Search each building name weekly on Willhaben + ImmoScout.

This is how you catch rare units in Lind, Völkendorf, Warmbad, Landskron, Viktring, etc.
